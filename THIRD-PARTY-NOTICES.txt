DeviceHub: third-party programs and their licenses
==================================================

(27 Oct 2026) DeviceHub's own code is Shawn's. It ships some programs made by others, unchanged, and downloads a few
more from their makers only when you click to install them. Each is listed here with its version, license and the
source code for that exact version. The license texts are in helpers/licenses/ (and beside each tool in tools/).

SHIPPED INSIDE DeviceHub (the zip and DeviceHub-Setup.exe)
----------------------------------------------------------

OpenRGB 1.1 (tools/openrgb/), unchanged from its official Windows 64-bit portable zip
  License: GPL-2.0 (helpers/licenses/GPL-2.0.txt)
  Source for this exact version: https://codeberg.org/OpenRGB/OpenRGB/src/tag/release_1.1
  Official download: https://codeberg.org/OpenRGB/OpenRGB/releases/download/release_1.1/OpenRGB_1.1_Windows_64_8e16de2.zip
  Its zip carries these libraries, shipped with it unchanged:
  - Qt 6.8.3 (Qt6Core, Qt6Core5Compat, Qt6Gui, Qt6OpenGL, Qt6Widgets and the plugins in imageformats/, platforms/,
    styles/): LGPL-3.0 (helpers/licenses/LGPL-3.0.txt, which builds on helpers/licenses/GPL-3.0.txt). The DLLs are
    separate files and can be replaced with your own build of Qt 6.8.3.
    Source: https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/6.8/6.8.3/single/
  - libusb 1.0.27 (libusb-1.0.dll): LGPL-2.1 (helpers/licenses/LGPL-2.1.txt)
    Source: https://github.com/libusb/libusb/releases/tag/v1.0.27
  - hidapi 0.15.0 (hidapi-hotplug.dll): used under its BSD-style license (helpers/licenses/hidapi-BSD.txt)
    Source: https://github.com/libusb/hidapi/releases/tag/hidapi-0.15.0
  - PawnIOLib.dll, PawnIO's user-mode library: LGPL-2.1 (helpers/licenses/LGPL-2.1.txt)
    Source: https://github.com/namazso/PawnIO (folder PawnIOLib); the copy OpenRGB 1.1 ships:
    https://codeberg.org/OpenRGB/OpenRGB/src/tag/release_1.1/dependencies/PawnIO
  - PawnIO modules (tools/openrgb/board-modules/*.bin, used only for board lights): LGPL-2.1
    (helpers/licenses/LGPL-2.1.txt). Source: https://github.com/namazso/PawnIO.Modules; the copies OpenRGB 1.1 ships:
    https://codeberg.org/OpenRGB/OpenRGB/src/tag/release_1.1/dependencies/PawnIO
  - dxcompiler.dll and dxil.dll (Microsoft's DirectX shader compiler, version 10.0.19041, from the Windows SDK):
    dxcompiler.dll under the LLVM/University of Illinois NCSA license and the others in
    helpers/licenses/DirectXShaderCompiler-LICENSE.txt (source: https://github.com/microsoft/DirectXShaderCompiler);
    dxil.dll is Microsoft's own redistributable file, under the Windows SDK's license terms.
  The PawnIO driver itself is never shipped.

Equalizer APO 1.4.2 (tools/equalizerapo/EqualizerAPO-x64-1.4.2.exe), its official installer, unchanged
  License: GPL-2.0 (helpers/licenses/GPL-2.0.txt)
  Source: the project's code repository, https://sourceforge.net/p/equalizerapo/code/ (the 1.4.2 release)
  Official download: https://sourceforge.net/projects/equalizerapo/files/1.4.2/EqualizerAPO-x64-1.4.2.exe/download

Node.js (runtime/node.exe), the official Windows x64 build, unchanged. The version is named in runtime/LICENSES.txt.
  License: MIT, with the licenses of its own parts (V8, libuv, OpenSSL, ICU, zlib and others).
  Full text for v24.14.0: helpers/licenses/nodejs-v24.14.0-LICENSE.txt; for any version:
  https://github.com/nodejs/node/blob/<version>/LICENSE
  Source: https://github.com/nodejs/node (the tag of the version in runtime/LICENSES.txt, e.g. v24.14.0)

cue-sdk 4.0.84 (node_modules/cue-sdk), Corsair's iCUE SDK for Node, with Corsair's iCUESDK.x64_2019.dll
  License: MIT, Copyright (c) 2020 Corsair Memory, Inc. (its LICENSE file, also in runtime/LICENSES.txt)
  Source: https://github.com/CorsairOfficial/cue-sdk-node/tree/v4.0.84

node-addon-api 5.1.0 (node_modules/node-addon-api)
  License: MIT (its LICENSE.md, also in runtime/LICENSES.txt)
  Source: https://github.com/nodejs/node-addon-api/tree/v5.1.0

node-gyp-build 4.6.0 (node_modules/node-gyp-build)

DOWNLOADED FROM THE MAKER, ONLY WHEN YOU CLICK (not shipped by DeviceHub)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

  License: MPL-2.0. Source: https://github.com/LibreHardwareMonitor/LibreHardwareMonitor/tree/v0.9.6
PawnIO 2.2.0 (the driver for board, fan and RAM lights and the CPU sensors; its maker's official installer)

USBPcap 1.5.4.0 (for learning sessions; its official installer from GitHub)
  License: BSD-2-Clause for its programs; its driver GPL-2.0 or BSD-2-Clause (as its README states). Source: https://github.com/desowin/usbpcap/tree/1.5.4.0
ReaPlugs 2.36 (extra EQ plug-ins; DeviceHub opens its official page, https://www.reaper.fm/reaplugs/)
  License: Cockos' free license for ReaPlugs (freeware, not open source).
Keyboard layouts from VIA's library (usevia.app), fetched for a VIA keyboard that is plugged in
